# Logos

`easy-link-icon.png` (512px) is the app icon and favicon: a white and violet chain with three spark dashes. `easy-link-icon-ios.png` (470px) is the apple-touch-icon. Both are raster with their own shading; use them as-is at 24px and up, never recolor. The app bar shows the icon at 28px next to the "Easy Link" wordmark set in Manrope 800.
